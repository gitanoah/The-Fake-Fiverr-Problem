## Intro
 - I made a fiver account a couple days ago and once my government documents were confirmed and I posted a gig, I immediately got two emails on gmail saying "Hi <\username>, I've paid for <\service> and would like to proceed. Here’s the track link: [View link on Fiverr]" 
 - Basically saying a buyer placed an order and I needed to collect the funds. 

- Already getting a notification like this with only having my gig up for a couple hours raised my initial suspicion 

- It took me to an official “like” google doc and then a fake login page with “human verification”.
 - Which was what also made me realize this was a phishing attempt
